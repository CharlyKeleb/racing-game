# Race music credits

The in-race playlist (`RACE_MUSIC_TRACKS` in `src/audio/musicTracks.ts`). It is
in the spirit of the Need for Speed Most Wanted / Blacklist soundtracks:
energetic rock, drum & bass and electronic. Those games' actual songs are
commercially licensed recordings and **cannot** be shipped here.

Every file below was downloaded unchanged from [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org)
on 2026-10-21 and only renamed. Six are **CC0 1.0** (public domain, no
attribution required, credited anyway). "Race" by alex089 is **CC BY 4.0**:
it must stay credited, here and in the in-race "Race radio" card, which shows
the title, author and licence whenever a track starts.
