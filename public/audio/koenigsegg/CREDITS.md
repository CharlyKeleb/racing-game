# Koenigsegg One:1 audio credits

The One:1 uses a Koenigsegg Regera recording as a stand-in, replacing the
previous Mercedes S63 AMG recording. This is not a recording of a One:1.

Source: [Regera Goodwood 2018.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Regera_Goodwood_2018.ogg),
by **Mr JWW** — onboard acceleration at the 2018 Goodwood Festival of Speed.
[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N9ekHJ1PrR4).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
No endorsement by the original author is implied.

Changes: converted to 44.1 kHz mono PCM16, trimmed into three acceleration
segments, DC removed, level matched, and annotated with firing-pulse pitch marks
for the game's granular playback. The resulting `engine-bank.wav` and
`engine-bank.json` are generated by `scripts/audio/make_engine_bank.py` using
`scripts/audio/koenigsegg.json`. The original download is not shipped.

The short acceleration recording contains neither a steady idle nor a full
rev-range sweep. Layer RPM values are acoustic estimates, not telemetry.
Playback derives idle and upper revs by pitch-shifting these layers.
