# Koenigsegg One:1 audio credits

A stand-in until the One:1's own recordings are found: a real twin-turbo V8,
cut and level-matched into a grain bank with `scripts/audio/make_engine_bank.py`
(spec: `scripts/audio/koenigsegg.json`).

| File | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `engine-bank.wav` (idle, low and mid layers) | [S63 AMG V8 Engine Revs](https://freesound.org/people/marcelweiss/sounds/505321/), a Mercedes-Benz S63 AMG twin-turbo V8 revving in a car park | marcelweiss | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

The recording holds idle and short blips, so the layers stop at about 1900 rpm.
`GranularEngine` carries them up to the 8250 rpm redline. A crossplane V8 burbles,
while the One:1's flat-plane V8 screams, so on-board recordings of the real car
would be a step up.
